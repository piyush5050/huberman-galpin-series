# Huberman Lab Guest Series with Dr. Andy Galpin

AI-generated summaries and full transcripts of the six-part **[Huberman Lab](https://www.hubermanlab.com) Guest Series with [Dr. Andy Galpin](https://www.andygalpin.com)** on fitness, strength, endurance, recovery, and nutrition.

This is one of the densest, most signal-rich podcast series I've come across — six long episodes covering nearly everything that matters about training the human body. I made these summaries because I wanted to use the series to build my own optimal hypertrophy protocol with the help of AI, and I found the distilled versions easier to act on than the raw five-hour transcripts. There are other summaries of this series out there; these are simply the ones I made for myself, and I'm sharing them in case they help you too.

Each summary is a self-contained, visually designed page. **[Browse them live →](https://piyush5050.github.io/huberman-galpin-series/)** (or open any `summary-*/index.html` locally).
